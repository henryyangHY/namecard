Who is Henry

Beyond the resume: the story under the systems.

A resume tells you what I have done. This tells you why I keep doing it. My love of technology did not start with technology. It started with a very ordinary fear, and the first time a system set me free from it.

01 The one belief

Technology should make people more capable, not more busy.

That second half is autobiography. For years I was pathologically busy, stacking projects to prove I was worth something, until a system finally set me free. Now that line is not a slogan. It is the thing I rebuild for other people.

02 Origin

The scoreboard, and the system that broke it.

For a long time I believed my worth was a scoreboard: best grades, most projects, longest hours. At Taiwan's top high school I learned I would never out-study the natural geniuses, so I tried to out-work everyone instead, until the people around me told me my intensity was exhausting.

What actually saved me was a system. At seventeen I moved my student council's messy inventory to the cloud, and for the first time a win came not from being the smartest in the room, but from finding the simple logic under the chaos and turning it into something my whole team could run. Nobody stayed late counting boxes again.

Years later, applying to business school, the old anxiety came back and I nearly drowned in projects again. What pulled me out this time was not more hustle but a system: a second brain that traded overwhelm for clarity and control. It did not make me do more. It gave me my life back. So now I build that same relief for other people.
